Haldi & Bride Making

19th November · Bride's Residence

The haldi ceremony is a fun pre-wedding ritual in Indian weddings. Close family and friends gather to apply a bright yellow paste of turmeric (haldi), sandalwood, and oils to the bride. It blesses the couple, purifies their skin, and protects them from bad luck before the wedding day.
